1b applied to The Last Guest
Attendee screens stay on paper. The escape room is the only place the ink ground appears, so walking into it feels like walking into the flat. Graphite #1C2024 carries the phone card, and bone #C8CFC7 is the only thing that ever says "you may open it" — no second hue, so the section reads as a different room rather than a different brand.
Motion
One easing curve everywhere: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Screens rise 12px and fade in over 320ms, cards stagger in 40ms apart, every pressable thing scales to .97 under the thumb, the main button slides up from the bottom edge, and the unlocked phone button breathes a teal ring until it is tapped. Drag right on any screen to go back, with the screen following your thumb. All of it is off under prefers-reduced-motion.
What the bot sends
Four moments, one button each: 10 minutes before your game, your half at the door, game live (opens the phone directly), and each food item the moment the booth starts making it. Jam approvals and rejections too. Links are web-app buttons carrying startapp=phone or startapp=pass, so the app opens on the right screen with the user data the gate needs — and the server re-checks on arrival, so a forwarded link opens nothing.
New thing to decide: "your matcha is ready" needs a booth-side button to call it, which does not exist in the brief yet. Cheapest version is a Called button on the booth result card, which pings that one person.
